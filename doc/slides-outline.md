## Slide 2 — The Problem
- Millions of small sellers stare at raw metrics with no data team.
- Questions they cannot answer: Am I overpricing? Are reviews slipping? Is sales stalling?
- Existing dashboards show numbers, not judgment.
- Speaker note: mention the six input metrics come straight from platform seller consoles.

## Slide 3 — The Solution
- One input: shop metrics JSON. One output: a structured Health Report.
- Overall score 0–100, verdict (healthy / watch / critical).
- Six weighted dimensions with per-dimension explanations.
- Plain-language summary + prioritized recommendations, generated by a local LLM.

## Slide 4 — Architecture
- Diagram (left to right):
  Next.js dashboard -> POST /api/check -> Scoring API (rubric v0.2, deterministic)
  -> LLM layer: Qwen3-4B served by vLLM (OpenAI-compatible) on AMD Radeon Pro W7900D ROCm
  -> HealthReport JSON back to the UI.
- Highlight box: "Dual engine — rule-based scores always available; LLM adds prose when the GPU endpoint is up. engine=mock|llm."

## Slide 5 — The Scoring Rubric (rubric v0.2)
- Weights: price 0.2 · reviews 0.2 · salesTrend 0.2 · service 0.2 · listings 0.1 · activity 0.1.
- Verdict bands: overall >= 70 healthy, >= 50 watch, else critical. Dormant shops capped at 40.
- Example: price score from price position vs market average, with a "justified premium" bonus for 4.6+ rated shops.
- Speaker note: full formulas live in doc/scoring-rubric.md.
